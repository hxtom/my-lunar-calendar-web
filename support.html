Frequently Asked Questions

What does My Lunar Calendar show?

The app is designed to show Gregorian and Chinese lunar calendar information together, including traditional festivals and solar terms.

Can I use it for birthdays and reminders?

Use the app's available calendar and reminder features to keep important dates close at hand. Available functionality may vary by app version and device settings.

Why might calendar information differ from another source?

Lunar dates, holidays, observances, and regional conventions can vary by source or location. Please verify critical dates with an authoritative local source.

I'm having a problem with the app. What should I try?

Make sure you are running the latest version of the app and iOS/iPadOS. Closing and reopening the app, then restarting the device, can also resolve temporary issues.

How do I report a bug?

Please include your device model, iOS/iPadOS version, app version, a short description of what happened, and steps that reproduce the issue.
