Privacy Policy

Effective September 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how My Lunar Calendar handles information when you use the app.

Information and permissions

My Lunar Calendar may request access to device features or information when needed to provide functionality you choose to use. iOS and iPadOS control these permissions, and you can review or change them in your device settings.

Calendar and reminder information

If a feature uses calendar, birthday, reminder, or similar personal information, that information is used to provide the requested app functionality. The app should not be relied upon as the sole source for critical scheduling, legal, financial, medical, or travel decisions.

Data collection

The app does not intentionally collect personal information unless a feature clearly requires it or you voluntarily provide information when requesting support. If future versions introduce analytics, cloud services, advertising, accounts, or other data collection, this policy will be updated as appropriate.

Third-party services

The app may rely on Apple platform services and operating-system functionality. Those services are governed by their respective privacy practices.

Children's privacy

My Lunar Calendar is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data security

Reasonable care is taken in the design of the app, but no electronic system can guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the app changes. The effective date above will be revised when material updates are made.

Contact

For privacy questions, please use the developer contact information associated with My Lunar Calendar in the App Store.
